Chest
✓
DB Incline Press⭐ Start Here
3×10–12 · Prop on pillows/couch at ~30–45°. Upper chest is the biggest visual gap in your physique right now — prioritize this every push day.
✓
DB Floor Press
3×10–12 · Flat chest press from the floor. Eliminates shoulder hyperextension at the bottom — EDS safe. Good heavy chest volume.
✓
DB Chest Fly (Floor)
3×12–15 · Arms slightly bent, stop at 90° — don't let elbows drop below your torso. Squeeze hard at the top. Chest shape and inner detail.
✓
DB Pullover
3×12 · Lying on floor, single DB overhead arc. Hits chest + serratus + long head of lats in one — underrated multi-muscle aesthetic compound.
✓
Wide Push-Up
3×12–20 · Bodyweight or elevate hands on couch. Wide grip = outer chest. Can drape band across your back for added resistance.
✓
Band Chest Fly
3×15–20 · Anchor band behind you at chest height, fly arms together. Constant tension the whole rep — great chest pump and activation.
Shoulders
✓
DB Lateral Raise⭐ Start Here
3×15–20 · Most important shoulder exercise for aesthetics. Creates width and shoulder-to-waist ratio. Slight forward tilt, lead with elbows, don't shrug.
✓
Seated DB Overhead Press
3×10–12 · Seated on floor or firm surface. Stop just before lockout. Overall shoulder mass builder.
✓
DB Arnold Press
3×10–12 · Start palms facing you, rotate as you press overhead. Hits all three delt heads — good variety from standard OHP.
✓
Pike Push-Up
3×10–15 · Hips high, hands shoulder-width, lower head toward floor. Shoulder dominant. Good bodyweight OHP alternative.
✓
DB Front Raise
3×12–15 · Anterior delt. Rotate in occasionally — OHP already covers most of this, so don't do both on the same day.
Triceps
✓
Band Tricep Pushdown
3×20 · Anchor band at top of door, elbows pinned to sides. Low joint stress, great pump, easy to do anywhere.
✓
DB Tricep Kickback
3×12–15 · Elbow pinned, extend fully and squeeze at top. Slow negative. Best for that tricep "horseshoe" shape.
✓
DB Overhead Tricep Extension
3×12 · Single DB overhead, lower behind head. Long head emphasis — thickens the upper arm. Control the descent carefully.
✓
DB Floor Skull Crusher
3×12 · Lying on floor, elbows over face, hinge to lower DBs toward temples. Floor limits range safely — EDS safe. Light weight.

Back — V-Taper & Width
✓
Supported Single-Arm DB Row⭐ Start Here
3×10–12 each side · Knee and hand on couch. Drive elbow back toward hip — lat focus. Primary V-taper builder.
✓
Band Lat Pulldown
3×15 · Anchor band overhead at door top. Kneel or sit, pull elbows down toward hips. Creates lat width and that V-taper silhouette.
✓
Band Straight-Arm Pulldown
3×15–20 · Arms straight, slight forward hinge, pull band from overhead to hips. Pure lat isolation — incredible for V-taper. Rotate in with lat pulldowns.
✓
Chest-Supported DB Row
3×10–12 · Chest on couch at an incline, both arms row. Mid-back thickness. Neutral spine — no rounding. Good for those who want more back "3D" depth.
✓
DB Pullover
3×12 · Floor, single DB in overhead arc. Lats in long stretched position + serratus + chest. Can rotate between push and pull days.
Rear Delts & Scapula — Posture + 3D Shoulders
✓
Band Face Pull⭐ Start Here
3×20–25 · Anchor at face height, pull to forehead with elbows high and flared. Best exercise for posture, rear delts, and EDS shoulder health. Do every pull day without fail.
✓
Band Pull-Apart
3×20 · Band at chest, straight arms, pull apart until it touches your chest. Scapular retraction and rear delt. 60 seconds of work. Do these daily if possible.
✓
DB Reverse Fly
3×15–20 · Slight hinge, arms arc out and back. Rear delts make your shoulders look 3D from every angle — not just front-on.
✓
Serratus Punch / Push-Up Plus
3×15–20 · In push-up position at top, push scapulas apart (protract) then release. Builds the serratus "finger" muscles along the ribcage — an elite aesthetic marker that frames the abs.
✓
DB Shrug
3×15, 2 sec hold at top. Upper trap — adds the "athletic neck-to-shoulder" line. Don't roll the shoulders.
Biceps & Forearms
✓
DB Bicep Curl
3×12–15 · Supinate at the top (pinky up). Don't swing. Peak contraction squeeze.
✓
DB Hammer Curl
3×12–15 · Neutral grip. Hits brachialis — adds arm thickness that standard curls miss. Alternate with regular curls.
✓
DB Zottman Curl
3×10 · Curl up supinated, rotate to pronated, slow lower. Hits biceps up and forearms down. Time-efficient two-in-one.
✓
DB Reverse Curl
3×12 · Palms facing down. Brachioradialis and forearm — forearm development reads well on an athletic lean build.

Glutes — Highest Aesthetic Priority
✓
DB Hip Thrust⭐ Start Here
3×15–20 · DB or KB on hips, back on couch or floor. Drive through heels, squeeze glutes at the top and hold 1 sec. Zero lumbar compression. This is your #1 glute shape exercise — the mind-muscle squeeze is more important than the weight.
✓
Single-Leg Hip Thrust
3×12 each side · Same setup, one leg extended. Isolates each glute independently — great for symmetry and higher intensity.
✓
Band Clamshell
3×20 each side · Side-lying, knees bent, open against the band. Glute medius — hip stability and that outer glute "pop." Essential for EDS joint stability too.
✓
Lateral Band Walk
3×15 steps each direction · Band at ankles or above knees. Glute medius activation — directly counters your pelvic unleveling pattern.
✓
Standing Band Hip Abduction
3×15–20 each side · Band at ankles, kick one leg out to the side. Glute medius isolation standing. Can superset with clamshells.
✓
Band Glute Kickback
3×15–20 each side · Band at ankles, kick one leg straight back. Gluteus maximus isolation. Good finisher for the glute pump.
Quads & Compound
✓
KB/DB Goblet Squat
3×12–15 · Weight at chest, squat to parallel. Self-bracing for the spine — safest squat pattern. Can go heavier as back tolerance builds.
✓
DB Sumo Squat
3×12–15 · Wide stance, toes out, DB between legs. Adductor and glute focus, less knee stress. Good variation from goblet.
✓
DB Reverse Lunge
3×10–12 each side · Step back, not forward. Less hip flexion demand than forward lunge — easier on your right hip. Quad + glute together.
✓
DB Step-Up
3×10–12 each side · Sturdy step or stair. Drive through heel of top leg. Unilateral quad + glute — functional and effective.
Hamstrings
✓
DB Romanian Deadlift⭐ Start Here
3×12 · Hip hinge, neutral spine, feel the hamstring stretch — don't go past mid-shin. 40lb is solid. Progress when comfortable. Hamstrings frame the glutes from behind.
✓
Single-Leg RDL
3×10 each side · Light DB, balance and control matter more than weight here. Hamstring + glute + balance — progress slowly with EDS.
Calves
✓
Standing Calf Raise
3×20–25 · Off a stair edge for full range. Slow down, pause at the stretch. Hold DBs for resistance. Gastrocnemius.
✓
Seated Calf Raise
3×20 · Seated, DB on knees, raise up and hold. Hits soleus (deeper calf). Combine both for full calf development.
